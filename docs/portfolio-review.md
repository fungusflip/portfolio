# Portfolio review: 5 highest-impact improvements for a TA hiring manager

A hiring manager gives this about 30 seconds. They look for: can this person make something look good, do they build tools other artists use, and can they ship in my engine. Not implemented, only listed.

1. **Lead with a 30-second showreel and TA proof, not a drive-around.** The 3D game is charming but a recruiter on a laptop may leave before finding work. Put one autoplaying (muted) reel of 4-5 best clips (Camilla robots HDA, VAT fluid, painterly AnGame, grass RVT, BioYield explosion) at the top of the 2D site, with the CV button next to it.
2. **Show tools as tools: node graphs and parameter UIs.** For Camilla, Mutation Protocol and the farming tool, add a screenshot of the HDA network and the parameter panel next to the result, plus a short before/after clip. TAs hire people who build artist-facing tools; the "1 week instead of 4" number should be on the first screen of that page.
3. **Add shader and material breakdowns with the graph visible.** Water, foliage wind, painterly post-process: show the material graph (or HLSL snippet), and the cost (draw calls, instruction count, ms) where Filip has measured it. Performance numbers separate a TA from an artist. Measure them, do not invent them.
4. **Cut and reorder the project list.** 18 projects dilute the best 5. Mark 5-6 as featured with a one-line outcome each, and move early or school work (Cat Jam, Realistic Sword, Procedural Material) behind "more". Make Unreal and Houdini the visible tags.
5. **Make the CV and contact impossible to miss, and keep it current.** Ship the new TA CV (PDF and HTML), put email, GitHub and itch.io links in the header of every page, and close the loose ends a hiring manager notices: the TODO answers on project pages (C++ packaging, Lemon Lagoon tech art), a working "Download PDF", clear freelance vs jam vs school labels, and an honest dated status per project.

Also check: every video loads on a phone, and the page is usable without WebGL (the 2D site already helps).
