The environment for AnGame, a top-down-ish bow-combat roguelite in Unreal Engine 5.8 where you cultivate an organic heart while defending it in waves. Built with Houdini and Substance, then tied together in Unreal with a few small, controllable systems.

Procedural environment

The terrain pieces and scattered foliage are authored in Houdini, with textures from Substance. Scattering gives a dense, varied field without hand-placing anything.

Grass that blends into the ground

Scattered grass used to sit on the ground like a separate layer. I set up a Runtime Virtual Texture so the grass samples the ground colour underneath it. Two parameters tune the result:

  • GroundBlendHeight: how far up the blade the ground colour reaches
  • GroundBlendStrength: how strongly it is mixed in

Wind

I rebuilt the wind as one Material Parameter Collection, MPC_Wind, and two material functions, MF_Wind_Tree and MF_Wind_Grass. A single WindIntensity knob drives all of it, and LeafMovement adds noise to the canopy. Trees and grass share the same collection, so they always agree.

Painterly look

A full-screen post-process gives the whole world a painted feel: a 6x6 Kuwahara filter for the brush strokes and a Sobel pass for ink lines. A second Material Parameter Collection drives the day/night cycle and a per-day "mood" grade through these levers:

  • saturation and brightness
  • line strength and brush scale
  • haze amount
  • line colour, shadow tint and highlight tint

Problems I hit

After the upgrade to UE 5.8 the distant trees crawled. Two causes:

  • The default anti-aliasing had changed to TSR, and its jitter fed straight into the Sobel ink lines. I switched the project to SMAA.
  • The grass used a dithered opacity mask, which flickers when there is no temporal AA to smooth it. I turned it off on the foliage materials.
