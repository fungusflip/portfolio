AnGame is my bow-combat roguelite in Unreal Engine 5.8. You play from a top-down-ish view, grow an organic heart and defend it in waves. This page is about the world. I made the meshes in Houdini and the textures in Substance.

Walking through the field with the painted look on.

Scattering

I scatter the foliage, so nothing is placed by hand. The meshes are from Houdini (the cart too) and the textures are from Substance.

Grass that matches the ground

The scattered grass looked like a separate layer sitting on the ground. I set up a Runtime Virtual Texture so the grass picks up the ground colour underneath. Two settings tune it:

  • GroundBlendHeight: how far up the blade the ground colour goes
  • GroundBlendStrength: how much of it is mixed in

Wind

I rebuilt the wind as a Material Parameter Collection (MPC_Wind) and two material functions, MF_Wind_Tree and MF_Wind_Grass. One WindIntensity knob controls all of it, and LeafMovement adds noise to the canopy. Trees and grass read the same collection, so they never disagree.

Painterly look

Flying around in the editor viewport with the stats overlay on.

The whole screen goes through a post-process that makes it look painted: a 6x6 Kuwahara filter for the brush strokes and a Sobel pass for the ink lines. A second Material Parameter Collection drives the day/night cycle and a mood grade for each day. It controls saturation, brightness, line strength, brush scale, haze, line colour and the shadow and highlight tints.

Problems I hit

  • After the 5.8 upgrade the distant trees crawled. Anti-aliasing had switched to TSR, and its jitter went straight into the Sobel lines. I changed the project to SMAA.
  • The grass used a dithered opacity mask, which flickers without temporal AA. I turned it off on the foliage materials.
