The world for AnGame, my bow-combat roguelite in Unreal Engine 5.8. You play top-down-ish, grow an organic heart and defend it in waves. Meshes come from Houdini, textures from Substance, and a few small systems in Unreal tie them together.

Walking through the field with the painted look on.

Scattering

The foliage is scattered, so I don't place anything by hand. I made the meshes in Houdini, the cart included. The textures are made in Substance.

Grass that matches the ground

Scattered grass looked like a separate layer on top of the ground. I set up a Runtime Virtual Texture so the grass reads the ground colour under it. Two parameters tune it:

  • GroundBlendHeight: how far up the blade the ground colour reaches
  • GroundBlendStrength: how much of it is mixed in

Wind

I rebuilt the wind as a Material Parameter Collection, MPC_Wind, and two material functions, MF_Wind_Tree and MF_Wind_Grass. One WindIntensity knob controls everything. LeafMovement adds noise to the canopy. Trees and grass read the same collection, so they never disagree.

Painterly look

Flying around in the editor viewport with the stats overlay on.

A full-screen post-process makes it look painted: a 6x6 Kuwahara filter for the brush strokes and a Sobel pass for ink lines. A second Material Parameter Collection drives the day/night cycle and a "mood" grade per day. It controls saturation, brightness, line strength, brush scale, haze, line colour, shadow tint and highlight tint.

Problems I hit

  • After the upgrade to 5.8 the distant trees crawled. The default anti-aliasing had become TSR, and its jitter went straight into the Sobel lines. I switched the project to SMAA.
  • The grass used a dithered opacity mask. Without temporal AA it flickers, so I turned it off on the foliage materials.
