RoleFreelance: corridor, materials, Houdini tool
ToolsHoudini (HDA), stylized shaders, VFX

Freelance work for Camilla, a sci-fi game prototype. I made the corridor, the materials and a Houdini tool that builds rigged robots from sliders.

Problem

Making a new robot character took about 4 weeks.

What I built

A Houdini HDA that generates robot characters and rigs them automatically. It comes with a stylized shader library and VFX.

Result

A new character took about 1 week instead of about 4.

Robots

Every robot has the same skeleton, so one animation works on all of them. The synced dancing in the clip above is on purpose.

The generator (Houdini HDA)

Head size, limb length and proportions are sliders. The skeleton follows the mesh, and it exports straight to the engine.

Procedural hands

The tool generates and rigs the fingers, palms and joints. Hands are hard to get right, so they were my test of whether the system held up.

Corridor

Materials

Stylized metal, tech panels and organics. They share one palette so any mix still looks like the same game.

What I learned

Two lessons. Materials that match each other matter more than any single great asset. And a procedural rig needs hard limits on its sliders, or it breaks.
