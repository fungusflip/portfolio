A Houdini tool that generates grass, bakes it to cheap cards and brings it into Unreal with wind.

Wind data

Each blade stores its wind data in vertex colors:

  • R/G: position along the blade (0 root, 1 tip)
  • B: random phase, so blades don’t sway in sync
  • Amplitude: how much this blade sways

Generation

Sliders for width, clusters, blades per cluster, spread, height, bend and resolution. TOPs builds the variations in parallel. LODs and UV atlas packing are automatic.

In Unreal

Root-to-tip color gradient, subsurface and translucency. The wind shader reads the baked phase and amplitude. The grass bends away when the player walks through it.

Cards

Full grass meshes are too heavy for thousands of instances, so the tool bakes them to crossed quads with normal and alpha maps. The vertex colors survive the bake, so the wind looks the same.
