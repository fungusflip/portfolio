A farming environment pack: water VFX, a Houdini farmland tile tool and PBR props. Made for ALRPG, an action RPG prototype.

Problem

Farms of different sizes needed to be easy to place and quick to get into the engine.

What I built

A terrain tool in Houdini with anchor joints for placing farms. A grass shader where the crops grow through the vertex shader and move naturally. A water shader that shows the plants being watered.

Result

A farm is ready in the engine fast, with the growth and watering already working.

Water

Sine waves in the vertex shader, particles for foam, refraction, a depth color gradient and a Fresnel edge glow. Watering makes the carrots grow. Their growth is stored in vertex colors.

  • Vertex-shader waves
  • Foam particles
  • Refraction
  • Depth gradient
  • Fresnel glow

Farmland tiles

Sockets

Each tile comes with anchor points for crops, fences and tools, so props snap in place at the right height and angle.

Props

Full PBR sets: albedo, normal, roughness, metallic, AO.
