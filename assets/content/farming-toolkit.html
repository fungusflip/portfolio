A farming environment pack: water VFX, a Houdini farmland tile tool and PBR props.

Water

Sine waves in the vertex shader, particles for foam, refraction, a depth color gradient and a Fresnel edge glow. Watering makes the carrots grow. Their growth is stored in vertex colors.

Farmland tiles

Sockets

Each tile comes with anchor points for crops, fences and tools, so props snap in place at the right height and angle.

Props

Full PBR sets: albedo, normal, roughness, metallic, AO.
