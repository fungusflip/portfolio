A VR cyberpunk battle royale. I worked on it as a freelancer from August 2025, building procedural weapons and characters in Houdini, rigging and animating in Cascadeur, and integrating the VFX.

Freelance work: a Houdini tool that fits and drapes clothing onto any character without manual retopology. Parameters control how the cloth behaves. Designs made together with Elina Kans.

Problem

The art director wanted to play with weapon designs. Changing a weapon usually means redoing the rig and the animation, and that often breaks things.

What I built

A procedural weapon system in Houdini. The director changes the design with parameters and it is baked out for the engine. I also did the rigging and animation in Cascadeur and the VFX integration.

Result

Much faster iteration. The director can try designs without waiting for me.

Cloth tool: suits and hats

The nuke suit and the banana hat and glasses, as turntables.

Characters

All characters come from one base, and parts are added or removed with parameters. The rat zombie walks on its arms, so I renamed the bones to trick the auto-rigger, and it worked.

Improved workflow

I added a texture workflow to the HDA. It has texture parameters now, so I can try different colours and texture details quickly. The rat textures come from this. In the scene the rat is one node called "rat". It has a Geo tab and a Rat Texture tab, sections for Tail, Ears and Fingers (count, amplitude, radius) and an output path for the FBX export.
