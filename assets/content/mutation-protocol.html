Improved workflow

I added a texture workflow to the HDA. It now has texture parameters, so I can try different colours and texture details fast. The rat textures come from this. In the scene the rat is one node called "rat". Its parameters have a Geo tab and a Rat Texture tab, with Tail, Ears and Fingers sections (force count, amplitude, radius) and an output path for the FBX export.

In the second clip I move joints and rotate fingers directly on the green rat in the viewport, in Cloths.hiplc.

Textured rats

These are the rat variants with textures. They all come from the same base, so they share the rig and the animations. I made the textures through the texture parameters on the HDA, which let me try colours and texture details fast.
