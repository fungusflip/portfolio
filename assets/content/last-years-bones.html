Vines

Houdini HDA that grows vines as curves, with width and mask attributes. A solver changes the data every frame to grow them.

Lessons. Showing attributes as colors early made debugging much easier. I also learned not to merge a whole forest into one mesh: split it into batches, and fix texture density when you scale.

Serpent rig (Houdini → Unreal)

I modeled the head in Maya. The Houdini tool rigs and animates the body and sets its length, with procedural UVs. The body follows the head along a spline, lags behind it and banks in the curves. Exported both baked and as a runtime version.

Jitter fix. Smoothing the head path and using fewer spline points in tight curves got rid of the jitter.

Character rig

Steps: blockout mesh, small skeleton, geodesic skinning (shoulders and hips fixed by hand), then a walk test. I also tried volumetric skinning per joint.

I also modeled, rigged and skinned a humanoid with wings. The line look is Sobel edge detection on viewport textures.

Stone wall

First try vs. final. I exported masks from Houdini so the layers in Substance Painter came out right. Split normals at UV seams, and plan normals before baking.
