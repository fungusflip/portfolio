Team5 people, 4 weeks
ProcessScrum, Trello, Miro
TechState machine, observer pattern, ScriptableObjects

A four-week game made by five people, and my first full team project. You play Harmony the druid, fighting cursed critters to heal a forest. We worked in Scrum with Trello and planned in Miro.

What I did for the first time

Shaders that change the environment as you heal it, a state machine for quests, the observer pattern for events and ScriptableObjects for data.
