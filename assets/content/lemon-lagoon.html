Lemon Lagoon is a team game made for Comfy Jam: Summer. You serve refreshing drinks to thirsty guests and expand the menu as the party gets busier. It shipped as a jam build, and you can play it for free on itch.io.

My part

I made all the animations, in Cascadeur. I also did the rigging, the tech art and the implementation in Unreal Engine, and I packaged the shipped build, which involved C++.
