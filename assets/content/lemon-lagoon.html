Lemon Lagoon is a team game we made for Comfy Jam: Summer. You serve drinks to thirsty guests and add to the menu as the party gets busier. It shipped as a jam build, and it's free to play on itch.io.

My part

I made all the animations, in Cascadeur. I also did the rigging, the tech art and the implementation in Unreal Engine, and I packaged the shipped build, which took some C++.
