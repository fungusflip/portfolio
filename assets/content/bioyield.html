BioYield is a first-person survival shooter I'm building in Unreal Engine 5.8. I use it to practise tech art: VFX, materials, post-process and UI.

Smoke grenade

You throw a grenade and it bursts into a smoke cloud. The cloud is a Niagara system. The grenade is a Blueprint.

Explosion grenade

The other grenade is a volumetric explosion. I simulated a Pyro grenade-frag in Houdini, cached it to ZibraVDB and play it in Unreal. On top of that there is a camera shake, Niagara sparks and rock debris made of real meshes.

Drug effects as post-process

Drinking or taking something changes how the screen looks. Beer gives a drunk look and the hallucinogenic gives a colour-split trip look. The same system drives the low-health and winded (out of stamina) vignettes. I ported these from a Godot prototype.

Pickups and inventory

Items on the ground get an outline when you look at them. It is a post-process material driven by custom depth stencil, so only the item you aim at lights up. Press F to pick it up, then use it from the inventory.

Rations heal, beer makes you drunk for a few seconds, and the hallucinogenic starts the trip look. There is also a backpack screen with a grid, a character screen and a perk tree.

Debug keys

I needed a fast way to trigger every effect, so there is a debug keys panel in the corner. A number key turns on a vignette, G throws a grenade, and so on. Sprint is listed too, and the stamina bar at the bottom drains and refills as you run.

Still to show

Bullet impacts differ per surface (metal, flesh, concrete), with dust flipbooks for concrete and a blood mist material. The pistol has muzzle flash, smoke, ejected shell casings and a procedural recoil and slide. I also built a six-way lighting material from a SideFX Labs bake.
