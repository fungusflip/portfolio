BioYield is a first-person survival shooter I'm building in Unreal Engine 5.8. I use it to practise the tech art side of a game: VFX, materials, post-process and the UI that ties it together.

Problem

While building this I needed a fast way to trigger every effect and test each one on demand.

What I built

Two grenades (a Niagara smoke cloud and a ZibraVDB explosion), drug and vignette post-process looks, an outlined pickup and inventory system, and a debug keys panel.

Smoke grenade

Throw a grenade and it bursts into a smoke cloud. The cloud is a Niagara system, and the grenade is a Blueprint thrown from the player.

  • Blueprint

Explosion grenade

The other grenade is a full volumetric explosion. I simulated a Pyro grenade-frag sim in Houdini and cached it to ZibraVDB, then played it in Unreal. On top of that there is a camera shake, Niagara sparks and rock debris as real meshes.

Drug effects as post-process

Drinking or taking something changes how the screen looks. Beer gives a drunk look, the hallucinogenic gives a colour-split trip look. The same system drives the low-health and winded (out of stamina) vignettes. I ported these from a Godot prototype.

Pickups and inventory

Items on the ground get an outline when you look at them. It is a post-process material driven by custom depth stencil, so only the item you are aiming at lights up. Press F to pick it up, then open the inventory and use it.

Rations heal, beer makes you drunk for a few seconds, and the hallucinogenic starts the trip look. There is also a backpack screen with a grid, a character screen and a perk tree.

Debug keys

While building this I needed a fast way to trigger every effect, so the game has a debug keys panel in the corner. Press a number to turn on a vignette, G to throw a grenade, and so on. It also lists sprint, and the stamina bar at the bottom drains and refills as you run.

Still to show

Bullet impacts differ per surface (metal, flesh, concrete), with dust flipbooks for concrete and a blood mist material. The pistol has muzzle flash, smoke, ejected shell casings and a procedural recoil and slide. I also built a six-way lighting material from a SideFX Labs bake.
