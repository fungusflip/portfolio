An idle sim for phones in Unity/C#, early MVP (Aug 2025). Each villager has a personality (Soul based on the OCEAN model, Flame, Stone, traits, quirks) that a utility AI uses to choose what to do: relax, socialize or work.

Works now

  • Anonymous login that remembers you
  • Summoning with random tiers
  • Utility AI picks a need and a target tile
  • 2D NavMesh pathing over the Tilemap
  • Save state, mirrored to Firebase

Next

  • Crafting and economy
  • Pity and banners for summoning
  • Villagers that talk in their own style
