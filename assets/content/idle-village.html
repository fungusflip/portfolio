An idle sim for phones in Unity/C#, early MVP (Aug 2025). Each villager has a personality (Soul based on the OCEAN model, Flame, Stone, traits, quirks) that a utility AI uses to choose what to do: relax, socialize or work.

Village

The village is an isometric tile map. The captures are from the Unity 6.3 editor in a phone-sized Game view. There is a bottom bar with four buttons and a clock and a Delete button at the top.

Battle

There is a battle scene where wolves run around a field and fight the villagers. The text at the top says “Enemies remaining: 4/4”. The field is darker than the village.

Summon

The summon scene is a Lobby with a white pedestal and a Summon button at the bottom. Pressing it plays a blue particle effect above a ring. Then a card shows the new villager: a portrait, up to five stars and three ability icons.

Works now

  • Anonymous login that remembers you
  • Summoning with random tiers, shown as a star card (1 to 5 stars)
  • A battle scene where wolves fight the villagers
  • Building panels (Smithy, Inn and others) where you pick a villager
  • Utility AI picks a need and a target tile
  • 2D NavMesh pathing over the Tilemap
  • Save state, mirrored to Firebase
