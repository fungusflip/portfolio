An idle sim for phones in Unity/C#, early MVP (Aug 2025). Each villager has a personality (Soul based on the OCEAN model, Flame, Stone, traits, quirks) that a utility AI uses to choose what to do: relax, socialize or work.

Village

The newer builds, from the Unity 6.3 editor in a phone-sized Game view. The village is an isometric tile map with a bell tower, houses and a four-button bar at the bottom.

Crafting

Buildings open a panel. In the Smithy you pick an item and a villager, then press Auto or Forge. There is also a hold-the-bar minigame. When you clear it, a line says crafting gets a 1.5x bonus.

Expedition

You can send a party out. The screen shows “Enemies: 1/7 | Party: 3/3”, drops like “3x Grass” and a message when a slime is defeated. A Leave button takes you back.

Summon

The summon scene is a Lobby with a white pedestal and a Summon button. Pressing it plays blue sparks over the ring, then a card shows the new villager with up to five stars and three ability slots.
