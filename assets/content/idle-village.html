An idle sim for phones in Unity/C#, early MVP (Aug 2025). Each villager has a personality (Soul based on the OCEAN model, Flame, Stone, traits, quirks) that a utility AI uses to choose what to do: relax, socialize or work.

Village

The village is an isometric tile map. Buildings open a panel where you pick an item and a villager. The clips below are from the Unity 6.3 editor in a phone-sized Game view.

Battle

A battle scene where wolves run around a field and fight the villagers. The top of the screen says “Enemies remaining: 4/4”.

Summon

The summon scene is a Lobby with a white pedestal and a Summon button. Pressing it plays a blue particle effect over the ring, then a card shows the new villager with up to five stars.

Works now

  • Summoning with random tiers, shown as a star card (1 to 5 stars)
  • A battle scene where wolves fight the villagers

  • A crafting minigame
  • Utility AI picks a need and a target tile
  • 2D NavMesh pathing over the Tilemap
