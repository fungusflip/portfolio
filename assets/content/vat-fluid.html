A liquid sim in Houdini, baked to a Vertex Animation Texture and played in Unreal. The animation is by Philip Anderson (Maya).

Final: materials, synced animation and a spline camera.

WIP: camera and lighting.

Problems I hit

  • Maya, Houdini and Unreal disagreed on FPS and substeps.
  • Fresnel didn’t work on the translucent material until I set the lighting mode to Surface ForwardShading.
  • I had faked forces to keep the liquid in the cup, and the bake made every small error visible as jitter. UV precision, legacy VAT and cleaner forces fixed it.
  • On import, empty data in the textures broke the quads, and the plugin was broken in some Unreal versions. Lots of trial and error.
