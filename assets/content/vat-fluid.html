  • Vertex Animation Texture
  • Translucent material
  • Spline camera

Problems I hit

Frame rate and substeps. Maya, Houdini and Unreal disagreed on FPS and substeps.
Fresnel. Fresnel didn’t work on the translucent material until I set the lighting mode to Surface ForwardShading.
Jitter. I had faked forces to keep the liquid in the cup, and the bake made every small error visible as jitter. UV precision, legacy VAT and cleaner forces fixed it.
Import. On import, empty data in the textures broke the quads, and the plugin was broken in some Unreal versions. Lots of trial and error.
