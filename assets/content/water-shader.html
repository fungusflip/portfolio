A water shader in Unity Shader Graph. This is the project that got me into shaders.

Final version: scrolling UVs, plus screen position for fake reflections.

Foam

The shader compares the water’s depth with the depth buffer and lerps in foam where they’re close. It’s cheap and looks right.

Depth color

Light fades the deeper it goes. View-space depth and an absorption value blend the surface color toward the deep color.

Surface

Two normal maps blended together, plus noise to break up the foam.
