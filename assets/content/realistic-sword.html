Project Overview

A stylized sword created with a focus on clean topology, procedural PBR textures, and striking visual appeal. This project explores the balance between fantasy aesthetics and functional design, featuring bold colors and sharp silhouettes that make the weapon instantly recognizable.

Design Philosophy

The sword’s design is from Berserk, fan art based on Guts’ sword “The Dragonslayer”.

Special attention was given to the blade’s gradient and edge highlights, the ornamental guard design, and the wrapped handle details. Each element contributes to the overall narrative of a hero’s weapon—powerful yet refined.
