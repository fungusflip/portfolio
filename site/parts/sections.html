About

Technical artist. I build procedural tools, shaders and rigs.

I sit between art and code: making the pipelines, materials and tools that let a team move faster and keep a look consistent. Most of my work starts as a Houdini network or a shader graph and ends up as something an artist can use with a few sliders.

I like clean systems, small reusable pieces and a good cup of matcha while they compile.

  • Technical Art

    Procedural generators, vertex animation textures, rigs and the tools that connect DCCs to the engine.

  • Art

    Characters, props, materials and mood: lighting, Substance Designer and stylised looks.

  • Programming

    C# in Unity, C++ with OpenGL, and tools, AI and systems in between.
